Peter Long of Fannin GA > James Marion Long of Fannin GA

James Marion Long born about 1833 likely in Burke Co North Carolina; He served in Co H in the 5th Mounted Tennessee Infantry (Union) during the Civil War. He is living in the household of his parents Peter and Nancy Long in 1870 Fannin census; He purchased the Long homeplace from his father in 1876 for only $20 likely indicating that he would then be responsible for the care of his elderly parents. His widow Matilda sold this land (Sec 7, lot 283) to son Peter Long in 1919. They were living at Rock Creek, Stockhill Fannin Co in 1880 and 1900; Census records show many different dates of birth. He applied for an invalid veteran's pension in 1912.

James Marion Long had a brother named Peter Long because according to the family history passed down Peter (P.E.Long) was named after his uncle Peter Long. P.E. Long and his wife Sarah Loretta Bailey left Georgia and went to Fort Payne Alabama where they are buried;

Peter Elbert Long

"His obituary described him as 'a clean, upright, Christian gentleman in every sense of the word; ' It also said he was survived by a brother, Oscar Long who lived at Kent, Ohio and a sister Alice Garrett of Kennesaw, Georgia."

married Sarah Loretta Bailey about 1914 in Fannin Co GA, married 16 years in 1930 census profile. She was born May 10, 1890 in Fannin Co GA, and died Dec. 29, 1987 at Fort Payne, DeKalb County AL

Loretta was the daughter of Stephen Calvin and Zilpha Jane Griffith Bailey. She was the wife of Peter E. Long, and mother of one son, Delton E.,who was killed in WW II. Loretta's father, Stephen, died in 1900 leaving Zilpha to raise the children alone. The building of the Blue Ridge Lake and Dam forced the family to relocate, and Zilpha and the boys moved to Polk County, Tennessee. Rutha stayed in Fannin county, Loretta moved to Alabama, and Theodocia moved to Huntington, West Virginia.
