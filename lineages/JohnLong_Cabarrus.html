I am researching my Great X5 Grandfather John Long b. 1758 (?) d. 3 MAY 1819 (Rowan County, NC). He married Mary (last name unknown) b. abt. 1759 d. 6 OCT 1820 (Rowan County, NC). My John Long appears numerous times in the court minutes for Cabarrus County beginning with its formation in 1793. My family lived near Cold Water Creek and Dutch Buffalo Creek and are buried in the Old Crystal Springs Presbyterian Graveyard located on the county line between Rowan and Cabarrus Counties.
