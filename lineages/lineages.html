Long Family of North Georgia
Lineages

 James Long Sr - died 1802 Cabarrus NC. His male children were:

  • James Long II - born about 1760, died 1842 McDowell NC. Family moved to Union, Gilmer and Fannin GA
  • Peter G. Long Sr - born about 1760, died about 1850, Macon NC
  • George Long - lived 1820 Cabarrus NC
  • William Long - lived 1810 Montgomery NC

    • Other Related Families

    • Charles R. Long - born 1809 Montgomery NC. Clearly related to the above group. Lived in Haywood NC and Union GA. Likely the son of William Long (above)
    • George Long - born about 1808; lived Tennessee and Cherokee NC. Married Naomi. Clearly related to the above group.
    • William Riley Long - born about 1825; lived Union GA and Cherokee NC. Married Lucinda. Clearly related to the above group.
      Other (apparently unrelated) Families of North Georgia
    • Josiah Long - family lived in Anson, Burke NC and Union GA.
    • James Thomas Long - family lived in Lincoln, Macon NC and Gilmer GA
    • Descendants of Ware Long of Virginia - family appears in Fannin GA 1880 census

     

      Other (apparently unrelated) Families of Western North Carolina
    • John Long - family lived in Cabarrus near James Long Sr.
