1. James R. LONG III b abt 1800

Married Julia (Uly) Cansler

James married Julia Cansler on 1 Apr 1827 in Burke County, North Carolina. Julia was born in 1810 in Lincoln County, North Carolina. Daughter of Conrad Cansler. She died about 1871 in Fannin County, Georgia about age 61.
