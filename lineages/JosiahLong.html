Submitted to Genforum by Mary Ann Nichols Nov 2009

Some very nice person sent me a copy of Mastin Long's bible record several years ago. The reason is that they believe my, William Martin's widow (and his 2nd wife) had remarried to Josiah Long. The Martin children are also listed in this bible. So, I believe Josiah Long was married to Rebeccah Ross Martin (daughter of Walter Ross)... which may well have been his second marriage. In the 1790 Anson, they are all listed there on the same page.

from the Bible which was said to be that of Mastin C. Long, and published 1843 in Boxton by B F Mussey; and was owned by Mrs. Mary Long Mills of Lilesville, NC:

MARRIAGES:
Mastin C. Long to Ann S. Cox, October 9, 1832
James M. Long to Hattie Dean, Dec 27, 1865

Mastin C. Long Nov 30, 1800;
Ann S. Long born November 8, 1808;
Jesse C. Long August 10, 1836;
James M. Long October 1, 1837;
Mariah Ann E. Long November 4, 1839;
Francis M. Long August 24th 1842;
Rebecca Jane Long August 24, 1841.
Then the Martin children are listed: (my note: these were children of William Martin who left an Anson Will in 1793)
Sarah Martin August 3, 1787;
Nancy Martin August 3, 1787;
James Martin October 7, 1788;
Mary Martin February 14, 1792.
William Long, son of Josiah Long and Rebecca, his wife, was born Dec 27, 1794,
Clement Long was born Dec 14, 1796,
Mastin C. and Francis Long (twins) were born Nov 30, 1800; Jesse Long March 1, 1867;
Mary A. Long Oct 2, 1868;
Walter C. Long Spet 24, 1871;

Deaths:

Sara McDuffie Sept 14, 1871;
Mastin C. Long Dec 12, 1878;
James Martin Long Mary 19, 1993 age 65 yrs and 7 months and 19 days;
