Haywood County, North Carolina
Formed 1808 from Buncombe County

1819 Mar 22. DB B p 30. Thomas Woodfin to Peter Long: $150. "Beginning on a hickory on Fosgeys (sp?) line, between the branch that Stephen White formerly lived on and the branch that said Woodfin now lives on..." Witnesses: Enoch Underwood and James Long. Ackn June 1819 court registered 12 Jun 1820. Acreage not listed, but description suggests a rectangle. Likely 150ac.

1820 Apr 20. DB B p 440. Micajah Eastas to George Hatfiled. 50 ac on Scotts Creek. test: Peter (X) Long, Benjamin E. Hatfield. Proven Oct 1827 court.

1820 - From a history of Jackson Co NC by Max R Williams, Editor. "....Much earlier in 1820, John Bryson, Andy Cathy, Oliver Painter, Pete Long, Thos Henson, Manson Shuler, Hugh Bryson, Silas Watson, William Watson and Enoch Underwood had organized a subscription school near Cullowee to guarantee the salary of the teacher Zeke Shuler who had been hired to teach their 16 sons and daughters in a small log house owned by Major Hamilton Bryson."

1822 Dec. 20, Land Entry #93 by Peter Long 50 acres on the waters of Scots Creek

1824 Dec. 11, Land Entry #199 by Peter Long 50 acres on the N. side of Scots Creek
