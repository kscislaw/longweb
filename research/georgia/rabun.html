Rabun County, Georgia
Formed 1819 from ceded Cherokee lands

Researcher's note: The Longs of Rabun County GA are descendants of Peter Long Sr. of Macon NC through his sons Henry G. Long and James R. Long

Also note James R. Long was a JP for many marriages in the 1840s & George W. Long was a JP for many marriages in the 1860s
