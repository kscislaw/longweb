Henry Long 0001000001-010011001 pg 128 (married Hannah Houser)

Henry Long's will was dated 5 Nov 1847, proven 2 Apr 1849, and the inventory
filed 25 Apr 1849. In it he names his "beloved wife" Esther, and children,
Christian, Abram, Jacob, Philip, John, Daniel, Fanny, Susan and Peter. His
wife, Esther, was living in Blount County in 1850 with Margaret Riddle, mother
of Henry's daughter-in-law Mary Riddle Long. Later, Esther went to Newton Co.,
MO, with her daughter and her family [Sally McCallie who m. James B. Russell].
