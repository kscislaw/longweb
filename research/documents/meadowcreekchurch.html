Meadow Creek Baptist Church Minutes
Stanly County, North Carolina
Formed 1841 from Montgomery County (west of the Pee Dee River)

Website note: Member's names are likely sourced from various lists contained within the minutes at different dates. However, the compiler of this edited work in 1961 clearly gleaned a few of the church member's names from the actual minutes rather than from a "members' list". He may have cross referenced the member's lists with the minutes and added the unlisted names.
